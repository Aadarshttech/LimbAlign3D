# Data Licenses and Attribution

This repository adheres to the licensing terms of all clinical datasets utilized. We do not re-host raw medical CT scans in this codebase. Automated download scripts and conversion utilities are provided for authorized users.

---

### 1. VSDFullBodyBoneReconstruction
* **Reference:** Fischer, M., et al. "A multi-center dataset of full-body post-mortem CT scans with bone segmentations." *Scientific Data* (2023).
* **Repository:** Zenodo Record [8302449](https://zenodo.org/records/8302449)
* **License:** Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International ([CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/))
* **Usage:** Primary training, validation, and testing dataset for lower-limb bone segmentation and mechanical alignment angle estimation.

---

### 2. TotalSegmentator CT v2
* **Reference:** Wasserthal, J., et al. "TotalSegmentator: Robust Segmentation of 104 Anatomical Structures in CT Images." *Radiology: Artificial Intelligence* 5.5 (2023): e230024.
* **Repository:** Zenodo Record [10047292](https://zenodo.org/records/10047292)
* **License:** Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International ([CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/))
* **Usage:** Out-of-distribution (OOD) test set and off-the-shelf baseline comparison for pelvic and femoral segmentation.

---

### 3. HFValid Femur Collection
* **Reference:** Zenodo Record [7555270](https://zenodo.org/records/7555270)
* **License:** Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International ([CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/))
* **Usage:** Out-of-distribution validation of femoral head sphere fitting and proximal anatomical landmarks.

---

### 4. CTPelvic1K
* **Reference:** Liu, P., et al. "Deep Learning to Segment Pelvic Bones: Large-scale CT Datasets and Benchmarks." *Medical Image Analysis* (2021).
* **Usage:** Independent testing of pelvic segmentation boundaries.
